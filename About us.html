About SmartEduX

AI-Powered Learning – Faster, Smarter, and More Effective.

Our Mission

SmartEduX is created with one core objective: to revolutionize the study experience for students. Powered by advanced Artificial Intelligence, SmartEduX automatically converts long educational lecture videos into structured, concise study notes, interactive quizzes, and key takeaways.

Whether preparing for university exams or reviewing online courses, SmartEduX enables learners to master complex concepts in minutes instead of hours—making learning simpler, quicker, and significantly more productive.

What We Offer

Instant AI Summaries

Extract structured notes, slide points, and core concepts instantly from any YouTube video.

Automated Quizzes

Test your understanding with AI-generated multiple choice quizzes created directly from lecture content.

Organized Study History

All generated notes and quiz results are securely stored for fast exam revision and offline download.
