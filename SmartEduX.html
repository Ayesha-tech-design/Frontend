Why Choose SmartEduX?

How does the Video Summarizer work?
AI speech-to-text integration.
SmartEduX takes video/lecture links, processes the audio through AI, and extracts time-stamped summaries, headings, and core details automatically.
What are AutoNotes?
Instant study guides for quick revision.
AutoNotes are structured, bullet-point study guides generated instantly from lectures, allowing students to skip manual writing and focus entirely on reading.
How do MCQs help in exam preparation?
Self-assessment and feedback.
The built-in MCQ Generator automatically creates relevant practice questions based on your study topics, helping you test your concepts with instant results.
Can teachers use this platform?
Curated playlists and student management.
Yes! Instructors can create video playlists, manage learning materials, track performance analytics, and share resources easily with their students.
Do I get certificates for completing courses?
Industry-relevant course verification.
Absolutely! Once you complete any of our specialized courses (like Freelancing or Web Development), you receive a certificate to showcase your skills.
