Verified Certificates

Unlock verifiable digital credentials upon course completion to showcase your academic achievements and technical skills to employers.

What is Verified Certificates?

The Verified Certificates module on SmartEduX validates a student's dedication and mastery over specific courses and AI modules. Every certificate generated is uniquely coded, secure, and easily shareable across professional networks like LinkedIn.

Secure ID

Unique verification hash prevents forgery or duplication.

One-Click

Instant export to PDF and direct sharing options.

How It Works

1

Course Completion

Finish all module requirements, assignments, and evaluations embedded within your SmartEduX course.

2

Automated Generation

The system automatically compiles your name, course title, completion date, and a unique tracking ID onto an official layout.

3

Showcase & Verify

Download the high-resolution badge or allow employers to verify authenticity directly using your portal link.
