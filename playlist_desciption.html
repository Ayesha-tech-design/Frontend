Smart Study Playlist

SmartEduX Learning Playlist provides an organized collection of course lectures, allowing students to watch videos in sequence, track progress, and enjoy a structured learning experience from beginning to completion.

What is AI Learning Playlist?

AI Learning Playlist is an intelligent feature of SmartEduX that organizes every course into a structured collection of video lectures. Instead of searching for lessons manually, students can simply follow the playlist and complete each lecture in the correct sequence.

The playlist keeps your learning organized, saves your progress, and works together with AI Notes, MCQ Generator, Quizzes and Certificates to provide a complete smart learning experience.
