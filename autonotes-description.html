What is AutoNotes?

AutoNotes is a core AI-powered engine integrated into SmartEduX. It eliminates the hassle of manual note-taking during lectures. By processing audio transcripts, video files, or raw text input, it intelligently structures key concepts into clean, reader-friendly summaries.
